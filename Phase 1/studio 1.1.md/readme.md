# Visual Prompt Studio 1.1: The Three Bishops

## Poster Pitch
Create a science fiction movie poster called “The Three Bishops.” The poster shows three mysterious people wearing long black capes in a futuristic city at night. They secretly punish people who hurt or abuse others and escape the law. The people who were victims are rewarded with three million dollars. The story is about justice, revenge, and freedom.

Use straight vertical lines from the tall buildings to make the Bishops look powerful. Also use curved and diagonal lines from their capes, the streets, and the lights to lead the viewer’s eyes toward the three Bishops.

The three Bishops should be the biggest shapes in the poster. Their pointed hoods and large capes should create dark, triangle-like shapes. The buildings should have mostly square and rectangle shapes. Use positive space for the Bishops, buildings, and people. Use negative space with the dark sky and shadows around the Bishops to make them feel alone and scary.

The Bishops should look large and heavy because of their big capes. Their capes should have a rough texture, while the buildings should look smooth and metallic. The streets should look wet and shiny.

Use strong contrast between the black Bishops and the bright red and white light behind them. This should make the Bishops the main focus of the poster. Use mostly black, dark blue, purple, red, and a little gold. The gold light near the people at the bottom should represent hope and freedom. Do not show graphic violence. Make the poster feel dark, mysterious, and intense.

## Vocabulary Breakdown

### Lines
Lines help guide the viewer’s attention and create movement. The vertical buildings make the Bishops appear powerful, while diagonal and curved lines direct the eye toward them.

### Shape
Shapes are the forms that create objects in an image. The Bishops create large triangular shapes, while the city uses square and rectangular shapes.

### Texture
Texture is how a surface appears to feel. The Bishops’ capes look rough and heavy, while the buildings appear smooth and metallic.

### Volume
Volume gives objects the appearance of having mass and occupying space. The oversized capes make the Bishops look large and imposing.

### Contrast
Contrast occurs when opposite visual qualities are placed together. The dark Bishops against bright red and white light make them stand out as the focal point.
