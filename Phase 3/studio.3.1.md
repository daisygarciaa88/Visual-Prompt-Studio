# Visual Prompt Studio 3.1: Historical Context

## Prompt 1

**Historical Theme:** Art as Divine Power

**Historical Period:** Ancient Egypt

**Artwork Used as Inspiration:** Khafre with the Falcon God Horus

**Medium:** Stone sculpture

### Visual Prompt
Create a stone sculpture inspired by Ancient Egyptian art. Show a powerful Egyptian ruler sitting on a throne with the falcon god Horus behind him. Use smooth stone, simple lines, and a balanced design. Make the ruler look calm, strong, and important. Use a large size and straight posture to show power. Add Egyptian clothing, symbols, and a crown. The sculpture should look serious and still, with very little movement.
The artwork should show how religion and government were connected in Ancient Egypt. Pharaohs were seen as powerful rulers who had a special connection to the gods. The purpose of the sculpture was to show the pharaoh's power and his connection to the divine. It also helped people remember the ruler and showed that his power was meant to last for a long time.
## Prompt 2

**Historical Theme:** Humanism and the Move Toward Science
**Historical Period:** Early Renaissance

**Artwork Used as Inspiration:** Giotto, Lamentation

**Medium:** Tempera painting

### Visual Prompt 2
Create an Early Renaissance painting inspired by Giotto's Lamentation. Show a group of people gathered around a person who has died. Use natural-looking human figures with clear facial expressions and body positions. Include a rocky landscape, a blue sky, and a simple background. Use lines, color, space, and balance to make the people the main focus. Make the figures look more realistic than the flat figures used in earlier medieval art.
The artwork should show the changes that happened during the Early Renaissance. Artists became more interested in people, emotions, nature, and making figures look more real. The purpose of the painting was still religious, but the artist also wanted viewers to feel the sadness and emotions of the scene. This shows how Renaissance artists began using more realistic details while still creating religious artwork.
